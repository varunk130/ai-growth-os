### Capture tips
- Before recording, open `/demo` once and click **Reset** so the guided run starts from a cold memory (cycle 1 = activation cliff).
- Disable OS notifications; full-screen the browser; hide bookmarks bar.
- If you need a calmer pace, the guided demo already narrates each step via the caption bar — pause voiceover and let it carry shots 3–4.
- For a silent/GIF version, shots 4, 5, and 7 are the three that tell the whole story.
